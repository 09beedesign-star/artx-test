## Style Prompt

Premium technology launch title card with a dark graphite canvas, precise chrome typography, cyan signal light, and restrained emerald accents. The mood is cinematic, focused, and high-end: more product keynote than nightclub. Motion should feel engineered and deliberate, with scan passes, soft reveals, and clean exits.

## Colors

- Canvas: #05070c
- Graphite panel: #111722
- Primary text: #f4f7fb
- Signal cyan: #53d7ff
- Deep emerald: #35f0a8
- Muted caption: #a8b3c7

## Typography

- Headline: sans-serif, heavy weight, compact uppercase spacing
- Caption: sans-serif, medium weight, high legibility

## Motion

- Use short, precise title entrances from 24-60px offsets.
- Use scan-line wipes and thin stroke reveals instead of bouncy movement.
- Keep all repeats finite and deterministic.
- Exit with a controlled upward drift and fade.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use generic blue gradients or default web colors.
- Do not make the frame busy with decorative blobs.
- Do not use playful easing, overshoot, or cartoon motion.
- Do not let subtitles cover the title or leave the safe area.
